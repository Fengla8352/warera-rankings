Daily Discord report

Get a short WarEra report in your Discord channel once a day, for the country or alliance you choose.

1. Make a webhook in Discord

  1. Open the channel's settings: Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks.
  2. Click New Webhook, name it if you like, and click Copy Webhook URL.
  3. Paste that URL below. You need the Manage Webhooks permission in that channel.

2. Choose the report

A first report is posted right away, so you can see what it looks like.

Your reports

Paste your webhook above, then show the reports that use it. You can remove them here at any time.

    What is stored

    To send the report, this site stores your webhook URL, the report you chose, the time of day and your time zone. The URL is never shown again or sent to anyone else. Anyone who has the URL can post in that channel, so keep it private. Delete the webhook in Discord, or remove the report here, and nothing more is sent. At most 3 reports per webhook.
